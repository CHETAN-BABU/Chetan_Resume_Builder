# Deep Research Workflow

## 1. Confirm eligibility

Read `AGENTS.md`, `config/profile.yml`, and `context/evidence.yml`. Stop if the role requires professional production AI/ML/GenAI engineering, software engineering, MLOps, hardware, or production-pipeline Data Engineering. An entry-level retrieval-application role may continue only when candidate projects are accepted.

## 2. Verify the posting

Confirm the specific role page is active and shows the company, title, Ireland location, description, and application route.

## 3. Research six axes

Use `workflows/modes/deep.md`:

1. Data and analytics strategy
2. Recent company changes
3. Analytics culture and team structure
4. Likely reporting/data problems
5. Market and domain context
6. Chetan's evidence-backed angle

For current claims, record source URLs and dates, explicit/inferred status, confidence, and linked requirement ID. Prefer the verified JD and authoritative employer sources.

Rank every resume-ready project in `context/evidence.yml`, including the 12
August additions, and select exactly two distinct real projects. Research may rank
evidence but cannot create candidate experience or imply work for the target
employer.

## 4. Assess competition and seniority

- Posting age and reposting signals
- Applicant count when visible
- Required years versus Chetan's dated experience
- Whether junior Data Scientist requirements accept academic projects
- Interview-process evidence from reliable current sources

## 5. Save the report

Save to the isolated job artifact folder as `company-research.md`:

```markdown
# Deep Research: [Company] — [Role]

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Track:** Analytics / BI Delivery / Applied Data Science
**Posting:** Active / Needs manual check
**Eligibility:** Pass / Conditional

## Data and analytics strategy
## Recent company changes
## Analytics culture
## Problems to solve

- Selected project ID:
- Selection rationale and limits:
- Gaps:
- Interview story:
- Resume emphasis:
```

Never use facts or metrics from `../backup/previous-backups/` or retired Version1/Version2 company briefs.
