# Mode: Deep — Company and Team Research

Use this for every eligible analytics, BI, or junior data-science role that proceeds to resume tailoring. High-priority roles may receive deeper research, but `company-research.md` is always required for a passed resume job.

## Research axes

### 1. Data and analytics strategy

- What business decisions does the team support?
- Which BI, data, warehouse, or analytics platforms are publicly documented?
- Is the role focused on reporting, self-service BI, insight generation, data quality, or modelling?
- What outcomes and stakeholders appear most important?

### 2. Recent company changes

- Relevant product, data-platform, leadership, partnership, or hiring news
- Funding, acquisitions, layoffs, restructuring, or hiring-freeze signals
- Changes within the last six months, with dates and sources

### 3. Analytics culture

- Centralized BI team, embedded analysts, consultancy, or product analytics
- Stakeholder model and expected communication style
- On-site, hybrid, or remote expectations
- Learning opportunities and realistic seniority

### 4. Likely problems to solve

- Reporting bottlenecks, manual reconciliation, dashboard modernization
- Warehouse migration, data quality, semantic-model, or governance needs
- What the job description implies Chetan would need to deliver in the first 90 days

For every problem signal, record:

| Field | Required value |
|---|---|
| Problem/initiative | Short factual paraphrase |
| Source | Verified JD or authoritative employer URL |
| Published/accessed | Exact date |
| Evidence class | Explicit / inferred |
| Confidence | High / medium / low |
| Relevant JD requirement | Requirement ID |

Prefer the verified JD and first-party employer sources. A third-party article may provide context but must not be the sole basis for claiming what the team is currently solving. Clearly label inference.

### 5. Market and domain

- Main products, customers, competitors, and regulated-domain constraints
- Pharma/FMCG/healthcare/finance relevance to Chetan's experience

### 6. Chetan's evidence-backed angle

- Rank every resume-ready project from `context/evidence.yml`, including the
  audited 12 August MSc, RAG, Power BI, and agent-workflow additions
- Select exactly two projects for the resume using the configured weights
- Academic evidence only when the role is junior/graduate data science
- One interview story
- Material gaps and an honest mitigation plan
- Explicit check that the role does not require unsupported professional production AI/ML/GenAI engineering

The selected project remains Chetan's prior professional, academic, or virtual work. Never describe it as built for the target employer. If no credible analogue exists, record `NO_STRONG_PROJECT_MATCH`, choose the closest honest evidence only if it still helps the application, and do not fabricate a case study.

## Output

Save to the job artifact folder as `company-research.md` only after verifying the role is active and eligible. Include the selected project ID, all ranked alternatives and scores, current source URLs/dates, and the explicit-versus-inferred label. Cite current sources and distinguish sourced facts from inference.
