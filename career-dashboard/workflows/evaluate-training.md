# Evaluate Training or Certification

## 1. Read the profile

Read `config/profile.yml` and `context/PROFILE-NOTES.md`. The training must support one of:

- Data/BI Analyst
- BI/Power BI Developer
- Junior/Graduate Data Scientist

Do not recommend AI-engineering, MLOps, software-engineering, or production-LLM training as a profile shortcut.

## 2. Verify current facts

Use the provider's official page to confirm:

- Exact course/certification name
- Whether it is active or retired
- Current syllabus, prerequisites, exam format, price, and renewal policy
- Whether a demonstrable project is produced

Do not reuse old prices or retired certification codes from repository examples.

## 3. Score

| Dimension | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Target-role alignment | 25% |
| Hiring signal in current Ireland postings | 20% |
| Builds a documented skill gap | 20% |
| Portfolio deliverable | 15% |
| Time and cost | 10% |
| Provider currency and credibility | 10% |

## 4. Compare with alternatives

Compare the training with:

- Applying to relevant roles now
- Completing a focused portfolio project
- Strengthening SQL/Power BI/Python evidence
- Completing the MSc thesis
- A different current certification

## 5. Verdict

Return `DO`, `TIMEBOX`, or `SKIP`, with:

- evidence and official links
- expected career benefit
- time/cost tradeoff
- one better alternative when applicable

Do not maintain a static “top certifications” list; re-evaluate against the live market and current provider information.
