# LinkedIn Outreach Workflow

Draft messages; do not send them.

## 1. Confirm role and evidence

- The role must pass Chetan's eligibility gate.
- Read `config/profile.yml` and `context/sources/career.md`.
- Select one relevant, defensible proof point.

## 2. Find suitable contacts

Look for:

- Head/Director/Manager of Data or Analytics
- BI Manager, Reporting Manager, or Analytics Lead
- Recruiter or Talent Acquisition partner assigned to the role
- A peer Data Analyst, BI Analyst, BI Developer, or Data Scientist

Do not default to Head of AI, VP Engineering, or Engineering Manager for an analytics/BI application.

## 3. Draft

Connection requests must stay within the platform's current character limit. Use:

1. A specific observation about the role/team
2. One relevant proof point
3. A low-pressure question

Safe proof examples:

- 300+ NielsenIQ reports over six months
- Contribution to 160 Power BI report migrations in three months
- Five-source AstraZeneca reconciliation dashboard
- PL-300 certification and enterprise Power BI delivery
- In-progress MSc thesis for a junior data-science role

Do not use the USD 5 million contract claim, sole ownership, or completed thesis results.

## 4. Output

Provide:

- One connection request
- One longer follow-up
- Two backup contact types and why they are relevant
- Character counts

Never include Chetan's phone number and never send outreach without explicit authorization.
