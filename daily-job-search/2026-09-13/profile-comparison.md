The project order weighs problem similarity, required-skill evidence, strength of evidence and domain/stakeholder relevance; project dates are often incomplete, so recency is not used to manufacture precision. All selections are active, registered projects with resume content. Candidate-only Power BI portfolio wording stays within visible dashboard scope. Company systems never become candidate skills by association.
